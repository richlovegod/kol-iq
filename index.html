資料怎麼來的How this was built

五個步驟，任何疾病領域、任何國家都可以照同一套重跑一次。 Five steps — the same method can be re-run for any disease area and any country.
1

文獻層Literature layer

以 Europe PMC 與 PubMed 檢索「SCA 相關詞彙 × 機構國別 × 2021–2026」，彙整作者、機構、產出量與作者位置（第一／通訊）。本版掃得 64 篇台灣掛名論文。 Search Europe PMC and PubMed for SCA terms × country affiliation × 2021–2026, then compile authors, institutions, output volume and author position (first / corresponding). This edition covers 64 Taiwan-affiliated papers.

2

試驗層Trial layer

用 ClinicalTrials.gov API 以疾病 × 國別站點拉出試驗與主持人；登錄未列主持人時，再查當地官方登錄系統補齊（台灣為 TPIDB，日本為 jRCT）。 Pull trials and investigators from the ClinicalTrials.gov API by condition and country; where the registry lists no investigator, fill the gap from the local official registry (TPIDB in Taiwan, jRCT in Japan).

3

組織層Organisation layer

病友協會與專科學會的理監事、醫療院所名單、年會議程。 Boards, referral rosters and annual-meeting programmes from patient associations and specialty societies.

4

動態層Activity layer

以當地語言掃近三年媒體：醫師受訪、獲獎、研究記者會、政策公告。 Sweep the last three years of local-language media: physician interviews, awards, research announcements and policy notices.

5

交叉合併與分層Merge and tier

四層證據交叉比對後分層，逐筆標註資料品質問題（職稱版本不一、拼音未確認、名單未查證）。存疑之處一律標示，不逕行認定。 Cross-check the four evidence layers, assign tiers, and annotate every data-quality issue (conflicting titles, unconfirmed romanisation, unverified listings). Where something is uncertain, it is flagged rather than asserted.
